What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is software that can interpret a goal, use connected tools and perform a sequence of actions within defined boundaries.

Can an AI agent use our existing systems?

Yes. Agents can be connected to APIs, CRMs, databases, knowledge bases, email and other business tools where appropriate.

Do you build fully autonomous agents?

We design autonomy around the risk of the workflow. Some tasks can run automatically while sensitive decisions can require human approval.
