Frequently asked questions

Answers before you start.

Clear expectations, practical scope and technology choices based on your requirements.

What is custom AI agent development?

It is the engineering of an AI-powered software agent around a specific business workflow, including its tools, knowledge, permissions, integrations and operating rules.

Can an AI agent work with our existing software?

Yes. Where APIs or approved interfaces are available, agents can be connected to CRMs, databases, help desks, email, internal applications and other systems.

Does every AI agent need to be autonomous?

No. The right level of autonomy depends on the workflow. High-risk actions can require human approval while lower-risk tasks can run automatically.
