Frequently asked questions

Answers before you start.

Clear expectations, practical scope and technology choices based on your requirements.

What business processes can AI automation handle?

Common opportunities include document processing, lead routing, customer support triage, reporting, data extraction, internal knowledge workflows and repetitive back-office tasks.

Is AI automation different from traditional automation?

Yes. Traditional automation follows predefined rules. AI automation can interpret unstructured information and make bounded decisions before passing work to deterministic systems.

Will people still be involved?

When a workflow needs judgment, approval or accountability, the automation can route the task to a person rather than forcing full autonomy.
